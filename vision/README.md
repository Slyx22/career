# Vision

**Help young South Africans prove they are ready for work, and help
organisations find them.**

Youth unemployment (15–34) was 47.4% in Q2 2026, about 5 million
people (Stats SA QLFS). Many of them have real skills that never show up
on their CV, and employers have no cheap, trustworthy way to find them.
We sit in the middle: free for young people, paid for by organisations.

## How it grows

```
 Young person checks CV ──► gets score + next steps ──► improves CV
          ▲                                                  │
          │                                                  ▼
 friends see it ◄── shares certificate (LinkedIn/WhatsApp) ◄── 70+ certificate
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
                         opt-in talent pool of verified 70+ candidates
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
                organisations pay to find and contact them ──► hires
                                                             │
                                                             ▼
               hire outcomes prove the score works ──► certificate is
                                                       recognised ──► more youth
```

Every step depends on the one before it. Organisations only pay if the
candidates are good, which only happens if the score is right. So
**getting the evaluation right comes first.**

## Roadmap

Each phase has an exit test. We don't move on until it passes.

| Phase | What we build | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Score you can trust (now) | CV benchmark that runs on every change; SA job titles; "Required in South Africa" checklist; score bands and the 70+ seal | Benchmark passes; 1,000 CVs checked; strong-vs-weak gap stays clear ([evaluation.md](evaluation.md)) |
| 2. Prove it with people | Consent-based feedback ("Did this match your experience?"); recruiter grading study on anonymised CVs | Scores agree with recruiter judgement (see targets in evaluation.md) |
| 3. Talent pool | Opt-in for 70+ certificate holders to be visible to organisations; profile controls; withdraw anytime | 500 opted-in 70+ candidates across at least 10 job titles |
| 4. First paying organisations | Employer dashboard: search by job, score band, province; contact through the platform; pilot with 3–5 organisations | 3 paying pilots, at least 1 hire, no privacy complaints ([employer-platform.md](employer-platform.md)) |
| 5. Recognised certificate | Outcome stats published; named partner organisations; "Viewed by employers" signals | Hire rate of 70+ holders measurably higher than below-70 ([recognition.md](recognition.md)) |

## Principles

1. **Free for young people, always.** The Employment Services Act bans
   charging work-seekers for placement; it's also the right thing to do.
2. **Never claim what isn't true yet.** "Recognised by employers" is a
   goal we earn with evidence, not a slogan. See [recognition.md](recognition.md).
3. **People own their data.** Nothing goes to an employer without the
   person's explicit, separate opt-in, which they can withdraw.
4. **The score must be fair.** Check it doesn't favour people from
   expensive schools, urban areas or one language. See [evaluation.md](evaluation.md).
5. **Keep the legal pages in step** with every feature
   ([../docs/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md](../docs/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md)).
