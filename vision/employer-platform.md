# Employer platform: how organisations will pay

**Product:** organisations search a pool of young people who chose to be
found, filtered by job, score band (70+), province and availability, and
contact them through the platform.

## Who pays, and for what (hypotheses to test with pilots)

| Customer | What they want | Pricing idea (ZAR, to test) |
|---|---|---|
| SMEs hiring entry-level staff | A shortlist of ready candidates without paying an agency fee | R500–R1,500 per role, or R299/month for 5 contacts |
| Corporates with YES / learnership programmes | Many pre-checked candidates for programmes | R15,000–R60,000 per year |
| Training providers and SETAs | Candidates who need specific skills; proof of improvement | Per-cohort licence |
| Universities / TVET career offices | Readiness reports for their students (no candidate sharing) | Per-student licence |

Young people never pay (Employment Services Act s15 bans placement fees
from work-seekers; it's also our principle).

## What we build, in order

1. **Opt-in**, separate from the analysis consent: "Show my certificate,
   job, score band, province and contact button to verified
   organisations." Off by default. Withdraw any time; withdrawal hides
   the profile immediately.
2. **Candidate controls:** see which organisations viewed you; choose
   which certificates are visible; pause.
3. **Verified organisations only:** company registration (CIPC), a named
   contact, acceptance of a data agreement (no on-sharing, use only for
   hiring, delete after use).
4. **Two-step sharing:** organisations see certificate details first; the
   CV and contact details are released only when the candidate accepts
   that organisation's request.
5. **Audit log** of every view, search and contact.
6. **Employer dashboard:** search, shortlist, contact, pilot billing.

## Legal must-dos before launch

This is a checklist for an attorney's review, not legal advice.

- **POPIA:** a specific, informed, voluntary consent for sharing with
  organisations; purpose limitation; operator agreements with
  organisations; breach process; Information Officer registered.
- **Employment Services Act 4 of 2014:** anyone recruiting and placing
  work-seekers may need to register with the Department of Employment and
  Labour as a private employment agency, and may not charge work-seekers
  any fee. Confirm whether a search platform counts as "placing", and
  register if so.
- **Employment Equity Act:** no filters by race, gender, age, disability
  or other listed grounds. Score bands are the only quality filter.
- **Consumer Protection Act:** employer marketing must not overstate
  what scores prove (see recognition.md).
- **Company:** register a (Pty) Ltd before taking payments, so contracts
  and liability sit with the company.
- **Terms and privacy:** new sections for organisations and for the
  talent pool, per `docs/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Pilot plan

1. Phase 3 first: get 500 opted-in 70+ candidates.
2. Approach 10 local SMEs and 2 YES-programme employers; offer a free pilot
   for 2 months in exchange for feedback and hire outcomes.
3. Convert to paid when at least one hire happens and the organisation
   would pay again.
